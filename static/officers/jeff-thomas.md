Jeff has studied with some of the most respected teachers in the country, and he has paid that experience forward through inspiring countless numbers of students and professional performers through teaching and mentoring. All of this (and much more) allows Jeff to reach out and serve the interests of our musicians on a variety of levels. Jeff shares a blended family of six children with his wife and best friend, Kathy Thomas."
